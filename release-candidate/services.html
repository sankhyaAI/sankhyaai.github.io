Causal inference and statistics

Design studies and analyse data to understand effects, differences, and uncertainty.

  • Design an experiment. Plan A/B tests and marketplace experiments, including sample size and spillovers between users.
  • Estimate effects from existing data. Study programme or product effects using identification strategies, propensity score and doubly robust methods, and sensitivity analysis.
  • Analyse surveys and repeated measurements. Estimate group differences and changes over time using mixed models and Bayesian methods.

Applied AI and machine learning

Build and validate models and applications for a defined business or research task.

  • Predict outcomes from your data. Develop and validate models for research and business questions, including customer lifetime value and retention using SQL data.
  • Help users find relevant information. Build semantic search and recommendation systems, and evaluate the relevance of their results.
  • Automate a repetitive workflow. Build agentic AI systems with document retrieval (RAG) and tool integrations, defined permissions, output checks, and human review.

AI evaluation and privacy

Assess model performance, bias, reliability, and the effects of privacy protection.

  • Compare models for your task. Design evaluation datasets and metrics, check for data leakage, compare candidate systems, and report uncertainty in their performance.
  • Assess bias and reliability. Examine subgroup performance, calibration, and failure patterns in the AI-assisted workflow your users will encounter.
  • Reduce sensitive-data exposure. Review data use and sharing in analytics and AI workflows, assess de-identification and disclosure risks, and select suitable protections, including differential privacy where appropriate.
