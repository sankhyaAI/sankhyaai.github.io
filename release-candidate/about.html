About Sankhya AI

We bring statistical expertise to research, business decisions, and AI development.

Sankhya AI is a consulting practice based in Hyderabad, India, working across causal inference, statistical modelling, machine learning, and AI evaluation.

The practice

From a question to useful evidence

Our work starts with the question you need to answer and the data available. We design studies, develop analyses, and build AI applications around that question.

Projects range from clinical prediction and survey analysis to donor response, road-safety evaluation, and AI-assisted content workflows.

Technical leadership

Vishesh Karwa, PhD

Managing Director

Vishesh leads consulting engagements and technical work. He is a statistician and independent researcher specialising in causal inference, experimental design, differential privacy, and statistical modelling.

He holds a PhD in Statistics from Pennsylvania State University. His experience spans research, teaching, and applied projects in healthcare, engineering, and AI.

Rigour in the analysis. Care in the details.

Each project starts with your goals, the context, and what a useful result would look like. We develop an approach suited to your project, combining statistical rigour with expert judgement. We work closely with you, refining the work through discussion and feedback, with a clear focus on practical value—whether that means informing a decision, advancing research, or improving a workflow.
